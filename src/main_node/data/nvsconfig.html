Clutch Servo Angles

These two are the servo's travel limits — the servo is clamped to them, so it can never be driven past whatever you set here.

Re-finding the stop after clutch wear: widen the limit past where you expect the stop, Save, then on Calibration ease the manual servo slider out while watching the clutch arm. When the arm stops moving, record that slider position here and Save. The servo has enough torque to break the linkage, so the value you record is what protects it.
